# Arrangement brief (tool-agnostic)

Give this whenever the words will be sung. It must work pasted into any
music generator or handed to a human composer.

- **Identity (one line):** genre/feel, tempo in BPM, metre (e.g. 6/8, 9/8
  as 2+2+2+3), key or mode/makam with one-word colour.
- **Voice:** gender/range, character (dry, close, breathy, raw).
- **Instruments:** 3-6, named plainly; what is absent matters too.
- **Dynamic curve:** section by section — empty / building / full / bare.
- **Delivery per section:** sung, half-spoken (not rap), chant, a cappella.
- **Avoid:** 4-8 styles that would pull the song the wrong way.
- **Listen-for list:** what to check in the first take, one change at a time.

Section tags go in the lyric, in English, in square brackets, with at
most 5-6 descriptive words; never put words of the lyric's language inside
a tag.
